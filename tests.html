What the colors mean

PASS matches the benchmark.

UNEXPECTED FAIL something that used to match no longer does — treat as a regression.

KNOWN FAIL a physically confirmed behavior this version doesn’t implement yet.

NOW PASSING a known failure now matches — remove its knownFail line.

PENDING needs a physical test; shows what Trestle does today, never counted as a failure.
